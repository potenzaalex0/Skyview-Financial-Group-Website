An independent firm,
built around our clients.

Skyview Financial Group is a fee-only, independent Registered Investment Advisor headquartered in Ponte Vedra Beach, Florida. We craft customized financial plans for high-net-worth individuals, business owners, and corporate executives — and we serve as a fiduciary, every day, for every client.

Who we are

Fee-only. Independent. Always fiduciary.

Skyview Financial Group is a fee-only, independent investment advisory firm — which means we are not forced by any parent company to use proprietary products or to steer clients into investments because they happen to be more profitable for the firm. We craft customized investment portfolios and financial plans based only on the needs of the client.

We do not utilize cookie-cutter, one-size-fits-all packaged investment portfolios or products. Our process is one of personalization, tailored specifically for you. In our capacity as a fiduciary, we always have your best interest at heart and fully disclose our compensation.

While our offices are in Ponte Vedra Beach, Florida and Morristown, New Jersey, we serve clients throughout the country. Our focus is comprehensive wealth management — coordinating investments, planning, taxes, retirement, and equity compensation as a single integrated practice.

What we stand for

A foundation of trust.

Our practice is structured around a small set of non-negotiable commitments. They are how we earn — and keep — the trust of the families and professionals we serve.

Fiduciary, every day

As a Registered Investment Advisor, we are legally required to act in our clients' best interests at all times. That standard does not turn on or off — it governs every recommendation we make.

Fee-only compensation

We are paid by our clients, and only our clients. No commissions on products. No hidden incentives. The advice you receive is impartial because the compensation structure makes it impartial.

Independent advice

We have no parent company directing what we sell. We are not captive to any insurance carrier, fund family, or broker-dealer. Our recommendations are based on your goals — nothing else.

Personalized planning

No cookie-cutter portfolios. No off-the-shelf packages. Every plan is built from your circumstances, obligations, and the life you are working to build.

Transparent communication

You will know what you are paying, why we are recommending what we recommend, and what tradeoffs every decision involves. Plain language. No jargon. No surprises.

Long-term partnership

A financial plan is not a one-time deliverable. We meet, recalibrate, and adjust as life unfolds — through promotions, sales, retirements, and the unexpected.

Our process

Listening first.
Then strategy. Then follow-through.

Our wealth planning process is driven by listening first, formulating a strategy collaboratively, and following through with disciplined advice and personal service. Every engagement follows the same disciplined arc.

i.

Discovery

A complimentary "Get Acquainted" meeting where we learn about your situation, your priorities, and the questions keeping you up at night. No pressure, no obligation. The goal is to determine whether Skyview is the right firm to help you — and whether we are a fit on both sides.

ii.

Analysis

We gather and review your full financial picture — investments, employer benefits, equity compensation, taxes, insurance, estate documents, and goals. From there we identify gaps, opportunities, and the most consequential decisions on your horizon.

iii.

Strategy

Together we build a written plan: investment allocation, tax-aware structure, retirement income design, equity compensation strategy, and the funding pathway for the goals that matter most. The plan is yours — not a template.

iv.

Implementation

We help execute — opening accounts at our institutional custodians (Fidelity and Charles Schwab), implementing the investment strategy, coordinating with your CPA and estate attorney, and putting the planning recommendations into motion.

v.

Ongoing partnership

The plan evolves as your life evolves. We meet regularly, monitor portfolios, rebalance with discipline, and revisit strategy when circumstances change. Year-end tax planning, equity comp decisions, life events — we are accessible when you need us.
