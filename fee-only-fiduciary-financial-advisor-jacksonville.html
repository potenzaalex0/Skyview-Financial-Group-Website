What "fee-only fiduciary" means for you

A commission-based broker is paid when you buy a product. A "fee-based" advisor can charge a fee and collect commissions. A fee-only advisor — like Skyview — is paid solely by clients, which removes the conflict of interest baked into the other two models. And as a fiduciary, we owe you an ongoing duty of care and loyalty under the Investment Advisers Act — brokers are subject to Regulation Best Interest, which applies at the point of a recommendation: your interests come first.

Frequently asked

Common questions.

What is a fee-only fiduciary financial advisor?+

A fee-only fiduciary advisor is paid only by clients — never through commissions or product sales — and is legally required to act in your best interest. This structure removes the conflicts of interest common to commission-based brokers.

What is the difference between fee-only and fee-based?+

Fee-only advisors earn nothing but the fees their clients pay them. Fee-based advisors can also earn commissions from selling products, which can create conflicts of interest. Skyview is fee-only — we accept no commissions, ever.

How do I verify that Skyview is a fiduciary?+

Skyview Financial Group is a Registered Investment Advisor registered with the SEC. You can review our Form ADV and registration on the SEC's Investment Adviser Public Disclosure website under CRD #310581.

Do you serve clients across Jacksonville?+

Yes. Our office is in nearby Ponte Vedra Beach and we work with clients throughout Jacksonville, the Beaches, Nocatee, and greater Northeast Florida, as well as nationwide.
