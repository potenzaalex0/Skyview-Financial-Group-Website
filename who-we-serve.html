Sophisticated needs
require thoughtful counsel.

Our practice is intentionally focused. We work with accomplished professionals and families whose financial lives have meaningful complexity — and where coordinated, fiduciary advice creates measurable, lasting value.

Skyview Financial Group serves clients across Ponte Vedra Beach, Jacksonville, Northeast Florida, and nationwide. Below are the four client situations where our fee-only, fiduciary RIA practice creates the most value — though every relationship is built around the specific person sitting across the table, not a category.

i.

High-Net-Worth Individuals & Families

Wealth at scale brings opportunity — and complexity. The decisions in front of high-net-worth families typically span multiple disciplines: investment construction across taxable and tax-advantaged accounts, multi-year tax planning, estate structures, charitable giving, and the coordination of advisors who too often operate in silos.

We serve as the integrating point. As an independent fee-only RIA, we have no proprietary products to sell and no parent company directing recommendations. That independence — combined with our fiduciary obligation under the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 — is what allows us to give advice that is genuinely impartial. Our team includes a Certified Private Wealth Advisor® (CPWA®), a credential awarded by Investments & Wealth Institute that is designed specifically for advisors serving high-net-worth clients.

For families, we coordinate planning across generations: education funding, generational wealth transfer, family meetings, and the gradual involvement of next-generation family members in financial decision-making.

  • Investments Customized portfolios with tax-aware location strategy and disciplined rebalancing
  • Taxes Multi-year projections, Roth conversions, charitable structures, loss harvesting
  • Estate Coordination with your estate attorney on trust funding, beneficiary structure, generational planning
  • Family Education funding, family wealth meetings, next-generation onboarding
  • Concentration Diversification of inherited or accumulated single-stock positions
  • Cash flow Lifestyle modeling, real estate decisions, major purchase coordination
ii.

Business Owners

For business owners, personal wealth and business wealth are deeply entangled — and the decisions you make in one domain inevitably ripple through the other. We work with owners across the lifecycle: from building the business, through liquidity events, into the post-sale chapter that follows.

Our planning addresses the situations specific to ownership: optimizing qualified retirement plans (401(k), profit sharing, defined benefit, cash balance), coordinating personal investment strategy with business risk concentration, planning for liquidity events, and structuring the transition into a different financial life after a sale or generational transfer.

Pre-sale, we focus on tax-aware structuring, retirement plan optimization, and personal wealth diversification. Post-sale, we focus on managing the influx of liquidity — investing it thoughtfully, structuring it tax-efficiently, and translating sudden wealth into a sustainable plan.

  • Qualified plans 401(k), profit sharing, defined benefit, cash balance plan design and optimization
  • Liquidity events Pre-sale planning, transaction-time decisions, post-sale wealth management
  • Succession Coordination with attorneys and CPAs on family or third-party transition
  • Concentration Diversification away from business risk as the company grows
  • Taxes Entity structure coordination, multi-year planning, charitable strategies
  • Personal wealth Investment strategy, retirement design, family planning alongside the business
iii.

Corporate Executives

Modern executive compensation is rarely just salary. It is a portfolio of equity grants, deferred compensation, performance units, supplemental retirement plans, and concentrated stock — each with different tax treatment, vesting schedules, and decision points. Coordinating these moving parts is a discipline of its own, and one we have built the practice around.

We help executives at public and pre-IPO companies make the high-stakes decisions that come with equity-heavy compensation: when to exercise ISOs and NQs, how to manage RSU vesting and tax withholding, whether to make 83(b) elections, when and how to diversify concentrated positions, how to structure 10b5-1 trading plans, and how to integrate equity comp into a broader retirement and tax strategy.

Beyond equity, we coordinate executive benefit elections — deferred compensation, supplemental retirement plans, executive disability and life insurance — and ensure they fit the broader plan rather than sitting on their own.

  • Equity comp ISO, NQ, RSU, PSU, ESPP planning and exercise strategy
  • Concentration Diversification of company stock through 10b5-1 plans, exchange funds, charitable structures
  • Tax planning Multi-year projections, AMT analysis, NUA strategy
  • Deferred comp Election analysis, distribution planning, integration with retirement strategy
  • Pre-IPO 83(b) elections, exercise financing, tax modeling on liquidity event
  • Benefits SERP, executive disability, life insurance coordination
iv.

Retirees & Pre-Retirees

The years immediately before and after retirement are arguably the most consequential financial period of your life. The decisions made in this window — Social Security timing, Roth conversions, withdrawal sequencing, healthcare coverage, asset allocation transition — compound for decades.

We work with pre-retirees to optimize the final accumulation years: maximizing tax-advantaged contributions, executing Roth conversion strategies during low-income windows, coordinating spouse benefits, and designing the income strategy that will follow. For those already retired, we focus on durable income, tax efficiency across distribution sources, RMD planning, and the integration of healthcare costs into the long-term plan.

For those navigating the transition itself — selling a business, retiring from a long career, deciding when and how to claim Social Security — we serve as the coordinating point that brings together the planning, investment, and tax decisions that arrive simultaneously.

  • Income strategy Sustainable withdrawal design across taxable, deferred, and Roth sources
  • Social Security Claiming optimization for individuals and couples
  • Roth conversions Multi-year ladder design during low-income windows
  • Healthcare Medicare coordination, IRMAA management, HSA strategy
  • RMDs Distribution planning, QCDs, beneficiary structuring
  • Allocation Transition from accumulation-phase risk to distribution-phase risk
