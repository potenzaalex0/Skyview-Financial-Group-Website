Your business is not your financial plan

For most owners, personal wealth and business wealth are deeply entangled — and dangerously concentrated. Our job is to build a financial life that doesn't depend entirely on one company: diversified, tax-efficient, and ready for whatever comes next, whether that's growth, a sale, or a transition to the next generation.

Why owners choose a fee-only fiduciary

As a fee-only Registered Investment Advisor, we earn nothing from commissions or product sales — only the fees our clients pay us. We're legally required to act in your best interest at all times. That matters most on the biggest decisions, where a broker's incentives and yours can diverge.

Frequently asked

Frequently asked

What does a financial advisor do for a business owner?+

A fiduciary advisor coordinates the personal side of ownership: diversifying wealth away from business concentration, tax-efficient structuring, retirement-plan design (401(k), cash balance), and planning for a future sale or transition — working alongside your CPA and attorney.

How much does a fee-only financial advisor cost?+

Fee-only advisors are paid only by their clients — commonly a percentage of assets managed or a flat/retainer fee — with no commissions or product sales. We'll walk you through our fee structure transparently in a first conversation.

What is the difference between fee-only and fee-based?+

Fee-only advisors earn nothing but the fees clients pay them. Fee-based advisors can also earn commissions from selling products, which can create conflicts of interest. Skyview is fee-only — no commissions, ever.

Do I need a financial advisor before selling my business?+

Yes — ideally one to three years ahead. Most tax-saving and estate strategies must be in place before a letter of intent. Early planning is where the biggest difference is made.
